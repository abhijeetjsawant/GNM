The hip-line row is the right *kind* of repair, but this card mis-describes the defect, charges the wrong anatomy, and then gates the root on the interpolation-hold constant rather than on the smoother that will actually move it.

1. **Classification is only half honest, and frame 113 falsifies the “109–119 = inward collapse” story.** Run 158–168 is not a pose. A rigid pelvis does not gain 40–63 mm of 3D width because the performer lies down; foreshortening is a 2D effect. The A–C pair at 139–140° is inside D8’s 150° ceiling (`sin 140° ≈ 0.64`), so the pair can support a stable triangulation of a *wrong* depth. That is compatible with a baseline stretch. It is not proof of one. The table already fights the “solid stretch run” wording: only six frames are off (158, 159, 163, 165, 167, 168), with in-band holes at 160–162 / 164 / 166. Frame 113 is worse: it sits inside the alleged collapse window at **355 mm** (+65 %), while smoothed width is **160.4 mm**. The raw cell is an outward spike; the delivery is an inward one. The range “125–174 mm” silently drops it. Frame 109 is not even in performer 1’s off list (first off is 110). Before any band, from files already on disk: take `(left_hip − right_hip)` and split it into the A–C baseline direction versus the plane perpendicular to it; a depth stretch lives on the baseline/bisector and barely moves `|hip_mid − root|` (here 84–94 mm vs 76.5). If camera D has *any* hip detection that triangulation dropped, the A–C point’s residual in D separates the two: a depth-stretched pair misses D, a truly wide pelvis should not. Photographs of 158–168 do the same job. Do not treat 140° as “therefore stretch.”

2. **Charging both hips is the shoulder-line convention copied onto a segment that has a parent.** `LANDMARK_PARENT` already has `left_hip`/`right_hip` → `root`, and `RIGID_LIMBS` already regularises `root→hip` and `left_hip→right_hip`. The length code itself says it cannot tell which endpoint is wrong and therefore charges the child, except the shoulder line, which has no parent. The hips are not that case. Frames 84–86 are the counter-example the card quotes and then ignores: frame 84 is left 83.5 mm from root vs right 126.6 (median 126.7); frame 85 is the other hip. A hip-line fire that NaNs **both** slots discards a femoral head that matched the take. Demote then *re-inserts the good hip’s inward-or-stable rays into the same solve as the bad ones*.  
   The opposite charge — fire `root→left_hip` and `root→right_hip` separately at 0.15 — would mark only the hip that moved, on exactly these frames, and would mark both on a symmetric collapse. The card refuses that because root–hip p5/p95 is −21 / +36 %. That spread is **not** labelled “off frames removed,” unlike the hip-line honest spread. The collapse frames are in it (left root–hip 77–226 mm). Using the contaminated series to forbid the more precise rule is circular. Recompute root–hip p5/p95 on the same honest mask as the hip line. If that honest spread is inside 0.15, charge per hip and do not ship the both-endpoints row. If it is still outside, the pelvis landmark is too noisy to be a length reference and the hip-line row is the remaining instrument — but then say that, and pre-register 84–86 as known over-charge, not as class (i). I would not ship both-hips until that honest root–hip table exists. Default if it is outside: hip line, both charged, with 84–86 called out; default if inside: per-hip `root→hip`, not the shoulder convention.

3. **The root prediction is ill-posed, and `±6` is the wrong horizon.** `MAXIMUM_INTERPOLATED_GAP_FRAMES = 6` is the *hold* after a slot the sequence solve never saw. Demote keeps rays, so those frames are candidates (`~direct & support ≥ 1`) and are written back only on recovered cells. Unfired frames keep their triangulation (`solve_sequence_positions` refuses to overwrite two-view points). The leak into neighbours is `_fill_and_smooth_positions`’ Savitzky–Golay window, `SMOOTHING_WINDOW_FRAMES = 9` (±4 at 30 fps), plus `project_generated_foot_contacts` translating the whole body when the root moves. `PELVIS_SMOOTHING_FRAMES` is 0, so Kabsch is per-frame: spreading the hips changes `R_hips` and therefore `_leg_root_offset` even when the midpoint does not. Symmetric collapse with `|mid − root|` stuck at 68–81 mm implies *successful* recovery can leave the midpoint put and still rotate the pelvis frame; failed recovery (rays win) leaves D8b’s root. Either way, “median `< 20` mm, never `> 45` mm” is a guess, not a mechanism. The 23 smoothed vs 30 raw count already fails `30 − 11 = 19`; the extra four are the smear the 1 mm clause will trip on. Pre-register instead: (a) D8b vs D8c root and hips in the **pelvis frame**, or after subtracting that frame’s hoist, so a re-hoist is not a body-wide 1 mm FAIL; (b) displacement vs distance to the nearest *raw* hip-line fire, with the SG window as the expected envelope, report-only outside it; (c) Kabsch `R_hips` delta on the fired frames, because that is how a still midpoint still moves the delivered root.

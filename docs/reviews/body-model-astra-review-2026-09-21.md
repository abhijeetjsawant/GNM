1. **O1 needs a valid MHR fixture and a genuine mean-geometry must-fail.** “The 16 D3 channels’ MHR counterparts” does not define that fixture. D3 independently multiplies left/right bone offsets and individual spine segments; MHR shares bilateral length parameters and distributes one spine-length parameter across several joints. Its raw channels are zero-centred, additive parameters—not multiplicative factors with mean 1. Drawing raw values in `[0.7, 1.3]` also exceeds several configured limits. Freeze the native channel list, factor-to-parameter conversion, representable donor motion, scored joints and aggregation before dispatch. [D3 perturbations](/Users/abhi_macbook/Projects/apps/AutoAnim/tools/compare/d3_skeleton_gate.py:376), [MHR parameter mapping](/Users/abhi_macbook/Projects/apps/AutoAnim/.cache/mhr/assets/compact_v6_1.model:292), [limits](/Users/abhi_macbook/Projects/apps/AutoAnim/.cache/mhr/assets/compact_v6_1.model:594).

   **Pinned offsets do not make the inverse solve trivially exact.** They make a sound model-consistency oracle when truth uses those same fixed offsets. The fitter must receive landmarks without the truth identity or pose, and its exported FK must recover the specified truth joints.

   The must-fail must hold **actual geometry at the mean while re-solving pose**, then demonstrably fail the same ≤1 mm predicate on the perturbed bodies. Zeroing the 68 identity channels alone is insufficient: MHR explicitly supplies duplicate length/width channels among the 136 “pose” channels. I verified that `scale_spine_length=0.5` and zero identity with `spine_length_flexible=0.5` produce identical joint positions. Specify how those freedoms are handled in the oracle and control. [Deciding model comment](/Users/abhi_macbook/Projects/apps/AutoAnim/.cache/mhr/assets/compact_v6_1.model:214).

Everything else below is **debt, not a dispatch hold**:

- **Precision/recall and inflation.** Report precision, recall and IoU per declared part and population, with their decision status stated. IoU penalises excess area, but permits recall gains to outweigh precision losses. The finished rerun demonstrates this: A/performer 1’s 3-pixel dilation raises IoU **0.7431 → 0.7962**, while precision falls **0.9479 → 0.8577**. [Undilated](/Users/abhi_macbook/Projects/apps/AutoAnim/artifacts/compare/d4-body/silhouette-fitted.json:7574), [dilated](/Users/abhi_macbook/Projects/apps/AutoAnim/artifacts/compare/d4-body/silhouette-fitted.json:10534). IoU alone supports a net-overlap claim; it does not establish absence of inflation. Under the present card, P/R are diagnostic. A precision-loss veto would require an explicit pre-registered amendment, not an invented merge-time rejection. The committed instrument already requires separate P/R reporting. [Deciding line](/Users/abhi_macbook/Projects/apps/AutoAnim/tools/compare/silhouette.py:37).
